⚙ Autonomous actions

The autonomous loop

From signal to safe action.

Instead of waiting for an operator to notice a dashboard alert, Sentinel continuously closes the gap between observation and remediation.

01 / OBSERVE

See everything

Telemetry arrives from Kubernetes workloads, nodes and services through metrics and event streams.

→
02 / PREDICT

Understand risk

InfraMind-style prediction and policy evaluation identify demand, anomalies and optimization opportunities.

→
03 / ACT

Change safely

Sentinel executes reconciliation, scaling, rollout or remediation only within configured safety boundaries.

→
04 / LEARN

Close the loop

Outcomes become feedback: success, failure, time-to-fix and agent performance improve future decisions.

What Sentinel automates

A platform, not another dashboard.

The system combines orchestration, observability, deployment safety and autonomous remediation into one operating model.

🧠

Predictive Autoscaling

Anticipate workload demand instead of reacting after saturation. Scale compute based on expected pressure and policy constraints.

Kubernetes · policy engine
🩹

Self-Healing

Detect unhealthy workloads and execute bounded recovery actions through reconciliation and health-aware automation.

reconciliation · health gates
🚦

Canary Deployments

Progressively shift traffic, observe health signals and automatically roll back when deployment quality crosses a safety threshold.

canary · rollback
🤖

AI Agent Orchestration

Agent Controller manages remediation tasks, heartbeats, queues and outcomes. PatchBot can analyze CI/CD failures and prepare fixes.

PatchBot · Agent SDK
🛡️

Policy & Guardrails

RBAC · Vault · mTLS

Observability

Prometheus metrics, Grafana dashboards and Kafka events provide a continuous view of workload health and controller activity.

Safety by design

Autonomy with brakes.

Sentinel is built around the idea that automation should be powerful enough to act and constrained enough to be trusted.

Deployment safety pipeline

PRE-CHECK
Policy evaluation — validate cost, quota, SLA/SLO and change-freeze constraints.
CANARY
Progressive rollout — expose a limited slice of traffic before wider execution.
HEALTH
Health gate — score latency, errors and workload signals continuously.
ROLLBACK
Automatic recovery — revert when health thresholds are breached.
